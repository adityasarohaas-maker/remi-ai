02

Remi lives on a tablet in their home. It listens all day, answers like an old friend, and never tires of the same question.

No new devices, no apps to learn. If they can talk, they can use Remi.

  1. 1

    The family sets it up

    Who's who, the daily routine, and the topics that need a gentle touch. About ten minutes.

  2. 2

    Remi keeps them company

    Chats, stories, reminders for medicine and meals, and help putting names to faces.

  3. 3

    The family hears how it went

    A short note each evening, and an alert straight away if something seems wrong.
